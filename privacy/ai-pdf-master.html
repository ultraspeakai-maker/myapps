Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 26, 2026 | Application: AI PDF Master (com.example.aipdfmaster)

Vectra Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the AI PDF Master mobile application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App and the choices you have associated with that data.

1. Data Collection and Use

AI PDF Master is designed with a "privacy-first" approach. We believe that your documents should remain private. The vast majority of operations occur locally on your mobile device.

1.1 Document Processing & Local Storage

Any PDF document you import, open, or edit within the App is processed entirely on your device. File details and chat histories are saved locally in a secure SQLite database on your device. We do not host, store, or have access to any of your documents or chat logs on our own servers.

1.2 AI Chat Feature (Google Gemini API)

When you use the AI chat feature to ask questions about your PDF document, text fragments from the document and your chat query are sent to the Google Gemini API to generate responses. This data is transmitted securely via HTTPS. According to Google's API terms, data sent via API is processed securely and is not used to train Google's models. No personal identifiers are attached to these API queries.

1.3 Optical Character Recognition (OCR)

The Optical Character Recognition (OCR) scanner processes images locally using Tesseract OCR. This processing is performed 100% offline on your device, and no image data is sent to external servers.

2. Permissions Requested

To provide full functionality, the App requires the following system permissions:

  • Storage / Media Access: Required to select, open, edit, and save PDF files on your device.
  • Internet Access: Required to communicate with the Gemini API for the document chat feature, and to load advertisements.

3. Third-Party Services and Advertisements

We use third-party service providers to help fund the development of this free app. These services may collect information used to identify your device:

  • Google AdMob: We show ads provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use device advertising identifiers (e.g., Google Advertising ID) and telemetry data to serve personalized or non-personalized advertisements. You can manage these advertising identifier preferences in your device settings.
  • Google Play Services: Used to run the application frameworks and store local backup preferences.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Since we do not collect or upload your document data or chat logs to any servers, we do not store your data. All files, history, and caches reside on your physical device. You can permanently delete all chat history and cached files at any time by clearing the App cache/data in your Android System Settings, or by using the reset buttons in the App's Settings Screen.

5. Children's Privacy

Our App does not address anyone under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from anyone. If you are a parent or guardian and you are aware that your child has provided us with personal data, please contact us.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy.
