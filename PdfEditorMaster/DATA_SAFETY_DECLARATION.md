# Google Play Store Data Safety Declaration - PDF Editor Master

This document details the exact answers for the **Data Safety** section in Google Play Console for **PDF Editor Master**.

---

## 1. Overview & Data Collection Summary

- **Does your app collect or share any user data?**  
  👉 **Yes** (Only anonymized Advertising IDs handled by Google Mobile Ads SDK / AdMob).

- **Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit?**  
  👉 **Yes** (Uses HTTPS/TLS protocol).

- **Do you provide a way for users to request that their data be deleted?**  
  👉 **Not Applicable** (The app does not create user accounts or store personal user data on external servers).

---

## 2. Data Types & Declarations

### A. Location
- **Collected:** No  
- **Shared:** No  

### B. Personal Info (Name, Email, Address, User IDs)
- **Collected:** No  
- **Shared:** No  

### C. Financial Info
- **Collected:** No  
- **Shared:** No  

### D. Files and Documents (PDFs, Images)
- **Collected:** No  
- **Shared:** No  
- **Processing:** Processed 100% locally on-device.

### E. Device or Other IDs (Advertising ID)
- **Data Type:** Device or other IDs  
- **Collected:** Yes (By Google AdMob SDK)  
- **Shared:** Yes (Shared with Google AdMob)  
- **Ephemeral:** Yes  
- **Required / Optional:** Required for ad serving  
- **Purposes:** Advertising or marketing, Analytics, Fraud prevention & security
