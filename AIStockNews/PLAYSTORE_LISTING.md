## Full Description
Welcome to **AI Stock News** – your fast, intelligent, and privacy-first Indian stock market news and institutional tracking application! Stay updated with live market prices, institutional buying/selling activities, and breaking market news aggregated from open sources.

### 🌟 Key Features:
- **Smart Money Tracking:** Track official institutional disclosures, bulk & block deals, and holdings changes for Mutual Funds, FIIs, DIIs, Promoters, and Large Investors.
- **AI Market News Feed:** Real-time financial news aggregation with AI sentiment indicators (Positive, Neutral, Negative) and live relative timestamps ("Just now", "2 mins ago").
- **Live Stock Search:** Instantly search Top 500 Indian stocks across NSE and BSE with live price flashes and daily percent change indicators.
- **1-Minute Automatic Background Refresh:** Keeps your market data and news feed refreshed every 60 seconds automatically.
- **Sticky Banner & App Open Ads:** Integrated with Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) for seamless, non-disruptive browsing.
- **100% Free & Informational Tool:** Direct access without complex logins or subscription walls.
