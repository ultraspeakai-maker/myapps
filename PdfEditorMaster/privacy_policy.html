Privacy Policy - PDF Editor Master

Effective Date: July 30, 2026

Welcome to PDF Editor Master. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data and documents. This policy outlines how our application operates on Android.

1. Local On-Device File Processing

PDF Editor Master is built as a local-first application:

  • Document Operations: Page rendering, text editing, merging, splitting, compressing, and page manipulation are executed locally on your Android device using native system code and Apache PDFBox.
  • OCR Recognition: Optical Character Recognition is powered by Google ML Kit and Tesseract locally. No document images or text scans leave your device.

2. AI Features & Privacy

  • AI document conversation, summarization, and query extraction operate seamlessly without requiring registration or user accounts.
  • We do not store your personal identity or document contents.

3. Data Collection

  • We do not collect personal identifying information (PII) such as names, phone numbers, or contact lists.
  • No user accounts, profiles, or sign-ups are required to use the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

PDF Editor Master uses Google AdMob to serve advertisements. AdMob may collect device identifiers and usage telemetry to deliver personalized or non-personalized ads in compliance with Google's policies.

5. App Permissions

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required for ad serving and AI network requests.
  • READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used to open and save PDF files on your device.
