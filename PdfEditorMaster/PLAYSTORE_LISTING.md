## Short Description (80 characters max)
Edit PDF text, perform 10-lang OCR, merge, split & chat with documents on-device!

## Full Description
Welcome to **PDF Editor Master** – the complete, privacy-focused, local-first PDF editor and document management suite for Android!

### 🌟 Key Features:
- **On-Device PDF Text Editor:** Edit existing PDF text directly, replace words, and format text seamlessly.
- **Multilingual OCR Engine:** Extract text from scanned documents and images in 10 languages (English, Hindi, Marathi, Tamil, Telugu, Gujarati, Bengali, Kannada, Malayalam, Punjabi).
- **Comprehensive PDF Converter:** 21+ PDF tools including Merge, Split, Compress, Rotate, Watermark, Crop, and PDF to Image conversion.
- **AI Document Assistant:** Summarize long reports, extract invoice details, and ask questions about your documents instantly.
- **100% Privacy Focused:** Files remain strictly stored on your Android device. No account sign-ups or logins required.
