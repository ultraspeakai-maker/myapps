1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

V Spy Camera - Unlimited Free Recordings ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting user privacy. All video recordings, captured audio, and generated video thumbnails remain strictly on your local device. No video files or personal data are ever uploaded, transmitted, or stored on external cloud servers.

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

Local Media Storage: All recordings are saved directly into your device's local MediaStore gallery under DCIM/SecretVideoRecorder. The app does not operate any external user database or cloud sync backend.

Google AdMob Integration: The App is provided 100% free with no recording limits, supported by Google AdMob ads. AdMob may collect non-personally identifiable technical advertising IDs and coarse location metrics for ad delivery. Read Google's Privacy Policy for more information.
