Privacy Policy - VP Video Player

Effective Date: August 13, 2026 | App Package: com.ultraspeakai.vpvideoplayer
Summary: VP Video Player processes all video playback, audio extraction, trimming, and folder organization 100% locally on your device. We do not collect, store, or transmit your private media files to any external server.

1. Information Collection and Use

VP Video Player is designed with a privacy-first architecture. All media processing happens strictly on your local hardware:

  • Media Files: Your videos, audio files, and folders remain exclusively on your device storage. No cloud uploads or remote data collection take place.
  • Advertising (Google AdMob): We use Google AdMob to display non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements. AdMob may collect standard non-personally identifiable diagnostic data (such as IP address, advertising ID, and crash logs) in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

2. Android Permissions Explained

  • Storage Access (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO): Required to scan and display local media files.
  • Foreground Service: Enables seamless background video/audio playback when your screen is off.
  • Notifications: Displays playback controls in your notification status bar.
  • Internet Access: Loads AdMob advertisements and enables local Wi-Fi cross-platform file sharing.

3. Data Security

Because all media files remain locally stored on your device, your data is protected by your Android device's built-in operating system encryption and sandbox security.
