# Privacy Policy for AI Stock News

**Effective Date:** August 2, 2026  
**Last Updated:** August 2, 2026  
**Developer Support Email:** ultraspeakai@gmail.com  

Welcome to **AI Stock News** ("we," "our," or "us"). We prioritize your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our Android application handles data, advertising, and internet connectivity.

---

## 1. Information Overview & Informational Purpose
AI Stock News is an informational stock market news and data tracking application. 
- **Not SEBI Registered:** We are NOT registered with SEBI (Securities and Exchange Board of India). All market data, news items, and institutional transaction tracking are compiled from open public sources.
- **Educational Tool:** This application does not provide personalized investment advice or financial services. Users should conduct their own independent financial research.

---

## 2. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, harvest, sell, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.
- **No Account Registration:** The application requires no user sign-up, login, or personal user profiles.
- **Local Preference Storage:** App settings and watchlists remain strictly stored on your local Android device via standard Android SharedPreferences.

---

## 3. Advertising (Google AdMob SDK)
We use **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to display banner advertisements and app open ads within the application:
- AdMob may process anonymized device identifiers (such as Advertising ID) and crash telemetry strictly to deliver ads, prevent fraud, and measure ad performance.
- For detailed information on Google's privacy practices, please review [Google’s Privacy & Terms](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 4. Network & Permissions
AI Stock News requires an active internet connection to operate:
- **`INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:** Required to check network connectivity, fetch real-time market data updates, and load AdMob advertisements.

---

## 5. Security
We prioritize user data safety by executing all app logic locally without storing user identity or credentials on external servers.
