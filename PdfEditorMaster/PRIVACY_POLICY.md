# Privacy Policy for PDF Editor Master

**Effective Date:** July 30, 2026  
**Last Updated:** July 30, 2026  
**Developer Support Email:** ultraspeakai@gmail.com  

Welcome to **PDF Editor Master** ("we," "our," or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our application handles your data when you use our local-first PDF utility and document management tools on Android.

---

## 1. Local & On-Device Processing
PDF Editor Master operates primarily on your local device:
- **Document Operations:** PDF rendering, page editing, text replacement, splitting, merging, rotating, compressing, and watermarking are executed locally on your device using native platform libraries and Apache PDFBox.
- **OCR Text Recognition:** Optical Character Recognition (OCR) is performed entirely on-device using Google ML Kit and Tesseract. None of your document pages, scanned text, or image files are uploaded to external servers.

---

## 2. Artificial Intelligence Features
- PDF Editor Master provides built-in AI document chat, text summarization, and query extraction capabilities.
- Processing is performed securely without requiring account registration, user logins, or personal API key configurations.
- Conversational features execute locally or via encrypted cloud inference without storing your personal identity or document contents.

---

## 3. Data Collection and Usage
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect, harvest, or store your name, email address, phone number, contacts, or location.
- **No User Account Creation:** The application requires no user sign-up, login, or user profiles.
- **Device Storage:** Files opened, edited, or created by the application remain strictly stored on your device storage unless you choose to share them via third-party Android intent apps.

---

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)
We use **Google AdMob** to display non-intrusive banner and interstitial advertisements within the app to support free development.
- AdMob may collect anonymized device identifiers (e.g., Advertising ID) and performance telemetry to serve relevant ads and prevent fraud.
- For detailed information on how Google handles advertising data, please review [Google’s Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 5. Third-Party Permissions
The application requests the following Android system permissions:
- **`INTERNET` & `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE`:** Required for displaying AdMob advertisements and processing online AI queries.
- **`READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` & `WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE`:** Required on supported Android versions to read PDF files selected by the user and save edited documents to device storage.

---

## 6. Security
We prioritize the security of your files by keeping all file processing, rendering, and OCR local to your Android device.

---

## 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy
We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any updates will be published within the application and on this documentation page.
