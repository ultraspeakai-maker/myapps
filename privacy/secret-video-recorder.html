Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 26, 2026 | Application: Secret Video Recorder (com.camera.secretvideorecorder)

Vectra Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Secret Video Recorder mobile application. This Privacy Policy details our practices regarding the permissions used, data safety, and collection of information when using this application.

1. Data Safety & Privacy Guarantee

Secret Video Recorder is designed with absolute user privacy in mind. We operate under a strict zero-transmission policy for media files:

  • Local Media Storage: All videos and audio recorded using this App are stored directly and exclusively on your device's local storage (external storage).
  • No Cloud Uploads: We do not operate any database servers to host your videos. We never upload, transmit, stream, or share your recordings with ourselves or any third party. Your media is 100% offline.
  • Local Settings: App preferences, scheduler configurations, and secure PIN settings are stored locally on the device.

2. Sensitive Permissions Explained

Due to the nature of background video recording, this App requires several sensitive permissions. We only request these permissions to facilitate the core functions of the application as triggered by you:

  • Camera Access (android.permission.CAMERA): Required to capture video streams. This permission is utilized in the background only when you explicitly start a recording session or activate a scheduled recording event.
  • Record Audio (android.permission.RECORD_AUDIO): Required to capture audio along with the video stream. This can be toggled off in the App settings if you wish to record silent video.
  • Write/Read External Storage: Required to create folders and save the generated MP4 video files directly onto your device so you can access them.
  • Foreground Service (FOREGROUND_SERVICE_CAMERA): Required by Android operating system constraints to keep the camera active and recording reliably when you exit the App, turn off the screen, or open other applications. A persistent, non-intrusive notification is displayed to ensure compliance with Android transparency requirements.
  • Schedule Exact Alarm (USE_EXACT_ALARM): Required to launch the recording process at the exact times you program using the Scheduler Screen.

3. User Consent & Transparency

Background recording is never initiated automatically without user intervention. A recording session is only launched under the following circumstances:

  1. When you manually press the "Record" button in the App UI or use the homescreen widget.
  2. At the exact dates and times you manually program in the Scheduler Screen.

4. Third-Party Advertising & Services

To keep the application free, we partner with advertising networks. These third-party services may access limited device telemetry to display ads:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner, interstitial, or app-open advertisements. Google AdMob may collect and process telemetry data including Google Advertising IDs, device hardware details, and location metadata to serve relevant ads. You can opt-out of personalized ads in your Android Settings.
  • Google Play Services: Required to run Android system dependencies and manage standard licenses.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because we do not collect or upload any files to external servers, we hold no user data to delete from our end. All videos reside on your device. You can permanently delete any video by using the Gallery Screen within the App, or by manually removing files using a file manager. Uninstalling the app and clearing its local data through Android settings will erase all stored configurations and cached media.

6. Children's Privacy

This App is not intended for use by children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.
