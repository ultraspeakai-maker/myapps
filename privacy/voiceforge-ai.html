Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 26, 2026 | Application: VoiceForge AI (com.ultraspeakai.maker)

Vectra Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the VoiceForge AI mobile application. This Privacy Policy outlines what information is collected, processed, and secured when you use our Text-to-Speech generation services.

1. Data Collection and Usage

VoiceForge AI prioritizes local-first computing. We collect minimal information needed to operate the core generation and audiobook features.

1.1 Text Inputs & Speech Synthesis

The text scripts you enter to generate speech are processed directly on your device using the Android system TTS engine or secure, transient API processing. We do not store, monitor, or harvest the text content or generated audio on our servers. Your scripts remain private to you.

1.2 Local Database Storage

The App saves your generation history, custom audiobook projects, credits, and favorite items locally in an encrypted App database on your device. This data is entirely offline and never synced to cloud servers unless you manually opt into standard Android Google Drive backups.

2. Permissions Requested

VoiceForge AI requests the following permissions to ensure a smooth user experience:

  • Write/Read External Storage: Required to save and export synthesized voice tracks as MP3/WAV files onto your device's downloads folder.
  • Internet Access: Required to verify credits (if applicable), sync with the system TTS models, and fetch ads.
  • Post Notifications: Used to notify you when a long audio synthesis worker completes in the background.

3. Third-Party Frameworks and Advertisements

To fund the free version of VoiceForge AI, we use Google mobile ads. These networks collect telemetry to function:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner and interstitial ads. AdMob collects information including your device's Google Advertising ID, location details (if enabled), and app interaction events. You can manage these parameters in your Google Account settings.
  • Google Play Services: Manages base Android SDK tools.

4. Data Retention and Account Deletion

All data (audiobooks, history files, and settings) resides on your physical phone storage. You can delete all generation history, cached audio tracks, and user databases at any time by:

  1. Using the "Clear History" button in the App's Settings Screen.
  2. Clearing App data via Android Settings -> Apps -> VoiceForge AI -> Storage -> Clear Data.
If you have created a profile for syncing credits or subscriptions (if active), you can request account deletion by filling out our Support form at vibhutipandey.in/contact.html#deletion.
