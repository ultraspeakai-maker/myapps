Privacy Policy

Last Updated: June 26, 2026 | Application: Free AI Art Generator (io.github.ultraspeakaimaker.freeaiartgenerator)

Vectra Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates the Free AI Art Generator mobile application. This Privacy Policy informs you about how user prompts, generated images, and telemetry data are handled when utilizing this creative suite.

1. Data Processing and Usage

Free AI Art Generator uses online neural network processing to convert text prompts into digital art. We process this information with maximum concern for your confidentiality.

1.1 Image Generation Prompts

When you type a text prompt to generate art, the prompt is transmitted securely (via HTTPS) to our AI generation endpoint. This prompt is used strictly to render your requested image. We do not store, catalog, or share your text prompts on our databases once the generation request is complete.

1.2 Generated Masterpieces

The resulting high-resolution images are returned to the App and stored locally on your device. We do not claim ownership, review, or store copies of your generated images on our servers. You hold complete ownership and commercial rights to all images you create.

2. Permissions Requested

To operate properly, the App requires the following permissions:

  • Write External Storage: Required to save your generated artwork directly to your device's Camera Roll or Gallery.
  • Internet Access: Required to contact the remote AI art servers for image generation, and to serve advertisements.

3. Third-Party Advertisements

To fund the hosting fees for our generative AI models, we serve mobile ads. These services collect device metrics:

  • Google AdMob: Serves banner and full-screen ads. AdMob collects location metrics (general IP location) and device identifiers (such as Android Advertising ID) to deliver relevant ad content. You can configure personalized ad preferences in your device settings.
  • Google Play Services: Standard framework libraries.

4. Data Retention

Since we do not store prompts or images, we maintain no database of your generated media. Your generation history is saved only inside the app cache on your local device. Clearing the App cache/data via Android Settings will permanently remove your creation history.
