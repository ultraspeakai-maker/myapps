# Privacy Policy for VP Video Player

**Effective Date:** August 13, 2026

UltraSpeak AI ("we", "us", or "our") operates the **VP Video Player** mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our application.

---

## 1. Information Collection and Use

### A. Local Media Storage & Processing
VP Video Player is designed as an on-device local media utility. All video playback, audio playback, video trimming, MP3 extraction, background playback, and folder management are performed **100% locally on your device**.
- We **do not** collect, store, or upload your private videos, audio files, photos, or media folder contents to any cloud server or third-party service.
- Your media files remain exclusively under your control on your device's local storage.

### B. Advertising (Google AdMob)
The App displays advertisements served by **Google AdMob**. To provide ads, Google AdMob SDK may collect non-personally identifiable diagnostic and technical data, including:
- Device identifiers (IP address, Advertising ID)
- General location data (country/city level)
- App performance and crash diagnostics

This information is processed in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy: [https://policies.google.com/privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

---

## 2. Permissions Used by the App

VP Video Player requests the following Android permissions strictly to deliver core media features:
- **READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE / READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_MEDIA_AUDIO**: To scan, organize, and play your local videos and audio files.
- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE**: To enable continuous audio/video playback when your device screen is off or when using other apps.
- **POST_NOTIFICATIONS**: To display playback controls in the notification drawer during background playback.
- **INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE**: To load Google AdMob advertisements and enable local Wi-Fi cross-platform file transfer.

---

## 3. Data Security

Because all media operations occur on-device, your media files are protected by your device's built-in operating system security. We do not maintain any external database containing your media or personal information.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy

VP Video Player does not knowingly collect personal information from children under the age of 13. The App is intended for general audiences.
