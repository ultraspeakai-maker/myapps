## 3. Full Description (Max 4000 characters)
```text
VP Video Player is a powerful, elegant, and feature-rich Android media player designed for smooth HD & 4K video playback. Inspired by classic MX Player design aesthetics with a clean white theme, soft blue folder accents, and red notification badges, VP Video Player gives you complete control over your local video and audio library.

⚡ KEY FEATURES:

• Premium MX Player White Theme: Experience a beautiful light mode layout with soft blue borders, dark titles, and red notification count badges on folder cards.
• Smooth HD & 4K Playback: Powered by Google ExoPlayer for hardware-accelerated playback supporting MP4, MKV, AVI, MOV, FLV, WMV, M4V, and 3GP.
• Background Video & Audio Playback: Continue listening to your favorite videos or music in the background with screen locked or while using other apps.
• Wi-Fi Cross-Platform Share: Instantly share videos wirelessly with iPhone, iPad, PC, Mac, or Android devices via built-in Wi-Fi portal & QR code scan.
• Lossless Video Trimmer: Trim and save 30-second or 60-second video clips quickly directly inside the app.
• Audio Extractor (MP3): Extract crisp MP3 audio tracks from any video file with a single tap.
• Smart Recycle Bin: Safely delete files into a local trash bin and restore them back to their original folder paths whenever you choose.
• 16 Supported Languages: Enjoy full localization in English, Hindi, Spanish, French, German, Japanese, Korean, Chinese, Arabic, Portuguese, Russian, Italian, Turkish, Indonesian, Vietnamese, and Bengali.
• Gesture Controls: Swipe vertical left for brightness, right for volume, and horizontal for double-tap 10-second seek forward/backward.
• Picture-in-Picture (PiP) Mode: Floating video window playback over other applications.

🛡️ PRIVACY & DATA SECURITY GUARANTEE:
Your media privacy is our top priority:
- All video and audio playback, trimming, and folder organization occur 100% locally on your device.
- Zero cloud servers, zero file tracking, and zero personal data collection.
- Full offline playback capability.

📱 PERMISSIONS EXPLAINED:
- READ_MEDIA_VIDEO / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Required to scan and play your local videos.
- FOREGROUND_SERVICE: Ensures smooth background video playback when screen is off.
- NOTIFICATIONS: Displays playback controls in your notification status bar.
- INTERNET: Required to load standard AdMob ads and Wi-Fi file sharing.

Project Source & Developer Details:
GitHub Repository: https://github.com/ultraspeakai-maker/myapps

Download VP Video Player today and enjoy your video library in crisp HD quality!
```
